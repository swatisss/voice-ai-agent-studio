Oakhaven Dental Care is a fictional UK dental group used for demos. All content is synthetic.

Oakhaven Dental Insurance pays back part of the cost of dental treatment at any UK dentist, including Oakhaven practices. It is separate from the Oakhaven Care Plan, which is a monthly membership with one practice.

## Insurance levels and annual limits

| | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | £12 | £22 | £35 |
| Routine care | 100% up to £150 a year | 100% up to £250 a year | 100% up to £350 a year |
| Restorative treatment | 50% up to £300 a year | 75% up to £700 a year | 80% up to £1,200 a year |
| Major treatment | Not covered | 50% up to £1,000 a year | 60% up to £2,000 a year |

Limits run per benefit year, which starts on your policy start date and renews every year on that date.

## What each category includes

* **Routine care**: check-ups, X-rays, hygiene appointments and emergency appointments.
* **Restorative treatment**: fillings, extractions (including wisdom teeth) and root canal treatment.
* **Major treatment**: crowns, bridges, dentures and dental implants. Implants are covered at Level 3 only.

## Waiting periods

Routine care is covered from the day your policy starts. Restorative treatment is covered after a 3-month waiting period, and major treatment after a 12-month waiting period. Treatment you need because of an accident is covered from day one at every level, up to £1,000 a year.

## What dental insurance does not cover

Oakhaven Dental Insurance does not cover cosmetic treatment such as teeth whitening or clear aligners, sedation, treatment your dentist had advised or planned before your policy started, or replacing a crown, bridge or denture less than five years old. Emergency dental treatment abroad is covered up to £250 a year; other treatment abroad is not covered.

## How to claim

Pay your dentist, then claim within 6 months of the treatment date in the Oakhaven app or online. Upload the dentist's itemised receipt showing the treatment, the date and the cost. Most claims are paid into your bank account within 10 working days. Claim numbers start with DC, for example DC-5531.

## Claim statuses

* **Received**: we have your claim and will review it within 5 working days.
* **More information needed**: we have written to you saying what we need; the claim is on hold until it arrives.
* **Paid**: the money has been sent to your bank account.
* **Declined**: the treatment is not covered; the letter explains why. You can ask us to review a declined claim within 8 weeks.

## Pre-treatment estimates

For treatment over £300, ask your dentist for a written treatment plan and send it to us before you start. We reply within 5 working days with how much your policy will pay, so there are no surprises. An estimate is not a guarantee: the final amount depends on your claim and your remaining limit.

## Joining, changing or cancelling

You can join any time; there is no medical questionnaire. You can cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund if you have not claimed. After that you can cancel any time with one month's notice. Moving up a level starts new waiting periods for the higher benefits only.

The Oakhaven Care Plan is a monthly membership with your Oakhaven practice that spreads the cost of routine care. It is not insurance: it covers set appointments and gives a discount on treatment, but does not pay back treatment costs.

## Care Plan options

* **Essential, £14.50 a month**: two check-ups and one hygiene appointment a year, and 10% off all other treatment.
* **Plus, £21 a month**: two check-ups and two hygiene appointments a year, 15% off all other treatment, and emergency appointment fees included.
* **Children**: children under 18 are covered free when a parent or guardian is on any Care Plan. They get two check-ups a year; hygiene and treatment are charged at the normal price less the parent's discount.

## Joining and cancelling a Care Plan

There is no joining fee and no waiting period: you can book your included check-up as soon as your first monthly payment has been made. There is no minimum term; cancel any time with one month's notice. Included appointments do not roll over to the next year if you don't use them. Late cancellation fees still apply to Care Plan members.

## Care Plan and dental insurance together

Many patients have both: the Care Plan pays for routine visits, and Oakhaven Dental Insurance helps with bigger treatment such as root canals and crowns. The Care Plan discount is taken off first, and you can then claim the rest from your insurance.
