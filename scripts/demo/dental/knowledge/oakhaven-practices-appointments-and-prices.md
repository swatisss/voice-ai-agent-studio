Oakhaven Dental Care is a fictional UK dental group used for demos. All content is synthetic.

## Our practices

Oakhaven Dental Care has three practices. All of them offer check-ups, hygiene appointments, fillings, root canal treatment, crowns, extractions, teeth whitening and emergency appointments.

* **Oakhaven Leeds**, 14 Wren Street, Leeds LS1 2QX. Phone 0113 496 0120.
* **Oakhaven Manchester**, 3 Mill Yard, Manchester M2 4WL. Phone 0161 496 0130.
* **Oakhaven Bristol**, 22 Harbourside Walk, Bristol BS1 5TY. Phone 0117 496 0140.

Dental implants, clear aligners and sedation are offered at Leeds and Manchester only.

## Opening hours

All practices are open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm. Oakhaven Leeds stays open until 8pm on Thursdays. Oakhaven Manchester and Oakhaven Bristol are also open on Saturdays from 9am to 1pm. Oakhaven Leeds is closed on Saturdays. All practices are closed on Sundays and on bank holidays.

## Out-of-hours emergency line

When the practices are closed, Oakhaven patients can call the out-of-hours emergency line on 0808 157 0123, every evening, weekend and bank holiday until 10pm. A dentist gives advice by phone and can book the first emergency appointment of the next opening day. If you are not an Oakhaven patient, call NHS 111 for urgent dental advice out of hours.

## Getting here and parking

Oakhaven Leeds is a five-minute walk from Leeds railway station; the nearest car park is Wren Street multi-storey (pay and display). Oakhaven Manchester has four free patient parking spaces behind the building; book one when you book your appointment. Oakhaven Bristol has no parking; Harbourside public car park is two minutes away.

## Accessibility

Oakhaven Leeds and Oakhaven Bristol have step-free access and a ground-floor surgery. Oakhaven Manchester is on the first floor with a lift. Every practice has a hearing loop at reception. We can book a British Sign Language or spoken-language interpreter at no cost if you ask at least 48 hours before your appointment.

## Becoming a patient

Anyone can register as a new Oakhaven patient, adults and children. Your first visit is a new patient examination (40 minutes, including any X-rays the dentist needs). You can register and book over the phone or online. Children under 16 must come with a parent or guardian.

## Contact

Call your practice during opening hours, or email hello@oakhaven-dental.example. Emails are answered within one working day. For anything urgent, phone rather than email.

## Booking an appointment

You can book by phone, online or at reception. Existing patients are asked for their patient ID and date of birth; new patients register first and book a new patient examination. Appointment lengths: check-up 20 minutes, hygiene appointment 30 minutes, new patient examination 40 minutes, emergency appointment 20 minutes, filling 45 minutes, treatment consultation 30 minutes.

## Appointment reminders

We send a text reminder 48 hours before your appointment and another on the morning of the appointment. Reply C to the first reminder if you need to cancel; there is no charge when you cancel at least 48 hours ahead.

## Cancellation policy

Please give at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment. Cancelling or moving an appointment with less than 48 hours' notice, or not turning up, is a late cancellation. The late cancellation fee is £30 for appointments up to 45 minutes and £60 for longer appointments. The fee is waived once a year, and always for a documented illness or emergency.

## Deposits for long appointments

Appointments of 60 minutes or more, and sedation appointments, need a £50 deposit when you book. The deposit is taken off your bill on the day. It is refunded if you cancel at least 48 hours ahead and kept if you cancel later or do not attend.

## Arriving late

Please arrive five minutes early. If you are more than 10 minutes late we will try to see you, but we may need to rebook you so other patients are not kept waiting, and this counts as a late cancellation.

## What to bring

Bring a list of any medicines you take and tell us about any change in your health since your last visit. Bring your dental insurance details if you would like us to fill in a claim form for you.

## Paying for treatment

Payment is due on the day of each appointment, by card or contactless; we do not take cheques. For treatment costing over £1,000 you can spread the cost over 12 months with 0% finance, subject to status. Before any course of treatment the dentist gives you a written treatment plan and estimate, valid for six months.

Oakhaven is a private dental practice; we do not offer NHS treatment. Prices below apply at all three practices. "From" means the final price depends on the treatment your dentist recommends, which is set out in your written estimate.

## Examinations and hygiene prices

* New patient examination, including any X-rays needed: £75.
* Routine check-up: £55.
* Hygiene appointment (scale and polish with the hygienist, 30 minutes): £75.
* Emergency appointment (assessment and temporary relief of pain): £85. Any further treatment is charged separately.
* Treatment consultation for implants, clear aligners or whitening (30 minutes): free.
* Children under 18 whose parent or guardian is on an Oakhaven Care Plan: check-ups free.

## Fillings and extractions prices

* White (tooth-coloured) filling: from £140 for a small filling, £190 for a large filling.
* Simple extraction: £150.
* Surgical extraction, including wisdom teeth: £325.

## Root canal treatment prices

* Front tooth: £395.
* Premolar: £495.
* Molar: £695.

Most teeth that have had root canal treatment need a crown afterwards; the dentist will tell you if yours does.

## Crowns, bridges and implants prices

* Porcelain crown: £795.
* Bridge: from £1,500.
* Single dental implant, including the crown: from £2,600. Offered at Leeds and Manchester only.

## Cosmetic treatment prices

* Home teeth whitening (custom trays and gel): £395.
* Clear aligners to straighten teeth: from £2,950, including the retainers. Offered at Leeds and Manchester only.

Cosmetic treatment is not covered by Oakhaven Dental Insurance.

## Sedation price

Intravenous (IV) sedation for nervous patients: £295 per appointment, in addition to the treatment. Offered at Leeds and Manchester only, after a free sedation consultation.
