## Home visits for housebound patients

Oakhaven offers home visits for patients who cannot get to a practice because of age, illness or disability. A dentist and a dental nurse visit homes and care homes within 10 miles of Oakhaven Leeds, Oakhaven Manchester or Oakhaven Bristol, on Tuesday and Friday afternoons.

A home visit costs £120, plus the price of any treatment. At home we can do check-ups, simple fillings, denture repairs and adjustments, and simple extractions; anything else is booked at the practice.

To book a home visit, call the patient's nearest practice. We ask about the patient's needs, their medicines and their GP's details, and a family member or carer can book on their behalf. Visits are usually available within two weeks.
