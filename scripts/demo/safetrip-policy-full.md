SafeTrip is a fictional insurer used for demos. All content is synthetic.

This is the policy wording for the SafeTrip Standard travel insurance plan: what it covers, the limits that apply, what is not covered and how to make a claim. All amounts are in US dollars and apply per insured traveller unless stated otherwise.

## Summary of benefits

| Benefit | Limit | Excess |
|---|---|---|
| Emergency medical expenses abroad | $250,000 per trip | $100 per claim |
| Medical repatriation | Covered in full when approved by the assistance team | None |
| Trip cancellation | $5,000 per trip | None |
| Lost baggage | $1,500 per trip, $300 for any single item | None |
| Delayed baggage (over 12 hours) | $200 for essentials | None |
| Gadget theft or accidental damage | $500 per item, $1,000 per trip | None |
| Rental car excess refund | $3,000 per trip | None |

## Emergency medical expenses abroad

SafeTrip pays emergency medical expenses while you are abroad up to $250,000 per trip. This includes hospital stays, doctor visits, prescribed medicine and emergency dental treatment to relieve pain. A $100 excess applies to every medical claim.

Injuries from extreme sports such as skydiving and bungee jumping are not covered, and neither are pre-existing conditions you did not declare when you bought the policy.

## Hospital admission abroad

In an emergency, go to the nearest hospital first. Then call the 24/7 assistance line on +1 555 0100 within 48 hours of being admitted, so SafeTrip can guarantee payment to the hospital. If you do not call within 48 hours, SafeTrip may not be able to pay the hospital directly and you may have to pay and claim the money back.

## Medical repatriation

If a doctor says you need to be flown home, SafeTrip arranges and pays for medical repatriation. Repatriation must be approved by the assistance team before you travel. SafeTrip does not pay for a journey home that you arrange yourself without that approval.

## Trip cancellation

If you have to cancel your trip before you leave, SafeTrip refunds your non-refundable travel costs up to $5,000 per trip. You are covered when you cancel because of illness, injury or death of a close relative. Cancelling because you changed your mind is not covered.

## Lost baggage

If your baggage is lost, stolen or damaged during your trip, SafeTrip pays up to $1,500 in total, with a maximum of $300 for any single item. Report lost baggage to the airline before you leave the airport and keep the airline's report for your claim.

## Delayed baggage

If your baggage is delayed by more than 12 hours, SafeTrip pays up to $200 for essentials such as clothes and toiletries. Keep the receipts for everything you buy.

## Gadget cover

Phones, laptops, tablets and cameras that are stolen or accidentally damaged during your trip are covered up to $500 per item and $1,000 per trip.

To claim for a stolen gadget, report the theft to the local police within 24 hours and attach the police report to your claim. Without a police report a theft claim cannot be paid.

Not covered: gadgets left unattended in a public place, and scratches that do not stop the device working.

## Rental car excess cover

If your rental car is damaged or stolen, SafeTrip refunds the rental company excess you had to pay, up to $3,000 per trip.

Not covered: drivers under 25, off-road driving, and damage while driving under the influence.

## How to make a claim

While you are abroad: for a hospital admission, call the 24/7 assistance line at +1 555 0100 within 48 hours. For a theft, report it to the local police within 24 hours and keep the police report.

After you return home: send the claim form plus receipts within 30 days of returning home. The documents you need depend on the type of claim, for example a doctor visit, an urgent care visit, a hospital stay or dental treatment. SafeTrip never promises that a claim will be paid before it has been assessed.

## General exclusions

SafeTrip Standard does not cover extreme sports like skydiving and bungee jumping, pre-existing conditions not declared at purchase, or travel to countries under a government do-not-travel warning.

## Contact SafeTrip

The 24/7 assistance line is +1 555 0100. Call it for medical emergencies abroad, hospital admissions and approval of medical repatriation.
