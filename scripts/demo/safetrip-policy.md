SafeTrip Standard plan covers emergency medical expenses abroad up to $250,000 per trip, with a $100 excess per claim.

Lost or delayed baggage: up to $1,500 total, maximum $300 for any single item. Baggage delayed more than 12 hours: up to $200 for essentials, keep the receipts.

Trip cancellation: refunded up to $5,000 if you cancel because of illness, injury or death of a close relative. Cancelling because you changed your mind is not covered.

To claim while abroad, call the 24/7 assistance line at +1 555 0100 within 48 hours of a hospital admission. Send the claim form plus receipts within 30 days of returning home.

Excluded: extreme sports like skydiving and bungee jumping, pre-existing conditions not declared at purchase, and travel to countries under a government do-not-travel warning.
