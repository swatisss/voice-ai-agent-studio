Gadget cover

What is covered

Phones, laptops, tablets and cameras that are stolen or accidentally damaged during your trip are covered up to $500 per item and $1,000 per trip.

How to claim

Report a theft to the local police within 24 hours and attach the police report to your claim. Without a police report a theft claim cannot be paid.

Not covered

Gadgets left unattended in a public place, and scratches that do not stop the device working.
