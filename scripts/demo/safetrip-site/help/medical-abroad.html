Medical help abroad

In an emergency

Go to the nearest hospital first. Then call the 24/7 assistance line on +1 555 0100 within 48 hours of being admitted, so SafeTrip can guarantee payment to the hospital.

Getting home

If a doctor says you need to be flown home, SafeTrip arranges and pays for medical repatriation. Repatriation must be approved by the assistance team before you travel.

SafeTrip is a fictional insurer used for demos. All content is synthetic.
