Rental car excess cover

What is covered

If your rental car is damaged or stolen, SafeTrip refunds the rental company excess you had to pay, up to $3,000 per trip.

Not covered

Drivers under 25, off-road driving, and damage while driving under the influence.

SafeTrip is a fictional insurer used for demos. All content is synthetic.
