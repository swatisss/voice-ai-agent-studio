Use cases for SafeTrip travel insurance (fictional, synthetic). Load them into any agent with:
`python scripts/import_use_cases.py --agent "<agent name>" --file scripts/demo/safetrip-use-cases.md`

## Lost or delayed baggage
Limits for lost, stolen and delayed bags, and what to keep for the claim.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- My suitcase never arrived. How much can I get back?
- My bag is delayed, what can I buy?
- What's the most you pay for one item in my luggage?

## Stolen or damaged gadgets
Gadget cover limits, the police report rule and what is not covered.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- My phone was stolen in Rome. How much will you pay?
- I cracked my laptop screen on holiday, am I covered?
- Do I need a police report for a stolen camera?

## Rental car excess
Excess refund up to $3,000 and the driver and driving exclusions.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- The rental car got damaged, will you refund the excess?
- I'm 23, am I covered for rental car damage?

## Medical emergency abroad
Hospital first, then the 24/7 line within 48 hours; repatriation needs approval. Someone in hospital now escalates.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- I'm in hospital abroad, who do I call?
- Do I need approval to be flown home?
- My husband was admitted to hospital in Lisbon an hour ago.

## Trip cancellation
Refund up to $5,000 for illness, injury or a close relative's death; not for a change of mind.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- Can I get my money back if I cancel because I'm ill?
- Can I cancel because I changed my mind?

## Making a claim
Deadlines and the documents to send, looked up by claim type.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- What documents do I need for a claim after seeing a doctor?
- How long do I have to send my claim?

## What is not covered
Extreme sports, undeclared pre-existing conditions, do-not-travel countries.

Channels: voice, chat
Sample phrases:
- I hurt my back skydiving, is that covered?
- Am I covered in a country with a do-not-travel warning?
