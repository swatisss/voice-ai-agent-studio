# Demo data

All data is synthetic. The seeder (`voiceai seed`) builds the database from these specs.

* [Evergreen Health](evergreen-health.md) - Tenants, persona library, the five agents, 18 tools, skills, knowledge map, use-case catalog and regression scenarios
* [Insurance Claims Agent](claims-agent.md) - The realtime-pipeline claims agent: persona, tools, skills, knowledge and scenarios
* [Members, policies and claims](members-and-claims.md) - Mock business records: members, plans, policies, benefits, claims, documents, renewals, onboarding, outreach lists, internal reference data
* [Call history](call-history.md) - The ~120 synthetic historical calls with outcomes, analyses, clusters and human resolution notes
* [Knowledge base](kb/) - Seed knowledge articles per tenant (imported as an OKF bundle)
