# Demo data

All data is synthetic. The seeder (`voiceai seed`) builds the database from these specs. The demo company is the fictional UK health insurer Northgate Health.

* [Northgate Health](northgate-health.md) - Tenants, front desk, persona library, the three agents, 25 tools, skills, knowledge map, use-case catalog and regression scenarios
* [Northgate members, policies, claims and pricing](northgate-members.md) - Mock business records: products and the rating table, members, policies, payments, claims, pre-authorisations, specialists, cash plans, Digital GP, documents, underwriting, seeded memory
* [Call history](call-history.md) - The ~120 synthetic historical calls with outcomes, analyses, clusters and human resolution notes
* [Knowledge base](kb/) - Seed knowledge articles per agent (imported as OKF bundles)
