# Overview

Most claims are decided within 30 days of receiving all documents. Simple claims, such as a paid-in-full office visit, are often decided in about a week.

## Stages

Received, in review, pending information, then paid or denied. Each stage change appears in the claim timeline.

## Payment

When the plan pays a provider directly, the member owes only the co-pay or coinsurance on the Explanation of Benefits. When the member paid the provider, the plan pays the member by bank transfer within 5 business days of the decision.

## A claim is late

If a claim has passed 30 days since all documents arrived and has no decision, a claims specialist reviews it. The agent offers to hand the call to a specialist.

## Denied claims

A denial states the reason and an appeal deadline. Appeals and disputes are handled by a person, not by the phone assistant.
