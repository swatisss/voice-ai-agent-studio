# Direct settlement

With a pre-authorisation code, the specialist or hospital sends the invoice to us and we pay them directly, usually within **10 working days**.

# Claim statuses

* **Processing**: we have the invoice and are checking it.
* **Paid**: we have paid the provider; any excess or shortfall is shown.
* **More information needed**: we are waiting for a report or invoice detail.
* **Declined**: not covered; the reason is given, for example an exclusion.

# Invoices sent to you

If you receive an invoice, send it to Northgate Health Claims, PO Box 4410, Leeds LS1 9NG, or upload it in the app, with your membership number and pre-authorisation code.

# Claim forms

When a claim form is needed, send it within 6 months of treatment.
