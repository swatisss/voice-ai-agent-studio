# What it is

For some conditions, members can call us directly and be assessed by our clinical team, then referred to a specialist or therapist with **no GP appointment needed**.

# Covered by Direct Access

* **Muscles, bones and joints**: back, neck, knee, shoulder or hip pain, sports injuries.
* **Mental health**: anxiety, low mood, stress, depression.
* **Cancer symptoms**: a new lump, unexplained weight loss, unexplained bleeding.
* **Moles and skin lesions** that have changed.

Everything else needs a GP referral first. Onward tests and treatment are still subject to your cover and exclusions.
