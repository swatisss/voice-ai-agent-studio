# Excess

The excess (£0, £100, £250 or £500) is paid **once per person per policy year**, on the first claim of the year. The hospital or specialist usually bills you for it directly. It resets at renewal.

# Shortfall

A shortfall happens when a specialist who is **not fee-assured** charges more than our fee guidelines. We pay up to our guideline amount and the specialist may bill you the difference. Choosing a fee-assured specialist avoids this.

# Disputes

If you think a bill is wrong, the claims team can check it with the specialist; disputes are handled by a person.
