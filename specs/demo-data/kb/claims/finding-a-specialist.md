# Recognised specialists

We cover treatment only by **recognised** specialists at hospitals on your **hospital list**. The assistant finds up to three near your postcode with their first available date.

# Fee-assured specialists

Fee-assured specialists agree to charge within our fee guidelines, so **there is no shortfall** for you to pay. Specialists who are not fee-assured may charge more than we pay.

# Video and phone consultations

Many specialists offer video or phone consultations, often sooner.
