# Steps

1. **Check whether you can use Direct Access.** For muscle, bone and joint problems, mental health concerns, cancer symptoms, and moles or skin lesions, call us first: you may not need to see a GP.
2. **Otherwise see a GP** (your own, or a Northgate Digital GP) and ask for an **open referral**.
3. **Contact us before any treatment.** Have ready: your membership number, your symptoms, when they started, when you first saw a GP about them, and the treatment the GP recommended.
4. We check you are covered and offer **two or three recognised specialists**. You can also choose a video or phone consultation.
5. If covered, we give you a **pre-authorisation code**. Show it to the specialist or hospital: they bill us directly.
6. **Call again before any further tests or treatment** the specialist recommends; each step needs its own pre-authorisation.

# Can I start treatment before you confirm cover?

No. Treatment without a pre-authorisation may not be paid.

# Medical reports

We may need your permission to ask your doctor for a medical report.
