# What it is

An **open referral** letter from your GP says the type of specialist you need (for example orthopaedics or dermatology) but does not name a particular consultant or hospital.

# Why it helps

We can offer you two or three recognised specialists available soonest. If your first choice is unavailable, you can see someone else without going back to your GP.

# When it is required

Open referral is mandatory on some company schemes. Children aged 17 and under need a referral to a paediatric specialist.

# Named referrals

If the GP names a specialist, check with us first that they are recognised and on your hospital list; otherwise the costs may not be covered.
