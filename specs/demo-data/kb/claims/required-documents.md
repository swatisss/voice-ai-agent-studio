# Overview

The documents needed depend on the kind of claim. Documents can be uploaded in your online account, emailed to the claims mailbox shown on your Explanation of Benefits, or posted. The claim number must appear on each page.

## Outpatient and urgent care

An itemized bill from the provider and proof of payment if you paid the provider yourself.

## Inpatient

The itemized hospital bill, the discharge summary, and the admission authorization number if the stay was planned. For an emergency admission, notify us within 48 hours.

## Dental

The itemized dental bill with procedure codes, and X-rays for crowns, bridges or implants.

## Motor: windscreen

The garage invoice and photos of the damage. No police report is needed for glass-only claims.

## Motor: collision

The repair estimate or invoice, photos of all vehicles involved, the other party's details, and a police report number if anyone was injured or the police attended.

## Missing documents

A claim with missing documents shows the status *pending information*. The decision clock starts when the last document arrives.
