# Main exclusions

* **Pre-existing conditions**, depending on your underwriting (moratorium or personal exclusions on your membership certificate).
* **Chronic conditions** that need long-term management, such as diabetes or asthma, once they are stable. We cover acute flare-ups.
* **Emergency care**: call 999 or go to A&E.
* **Normal pregnancy and childbirth**.
* **Cosmetic treatment**.
* Treatment at hospitals not on your list, or by specialists we do not recognise.

If a member disagrees with a decision, a colleague in customer relations can review it.
