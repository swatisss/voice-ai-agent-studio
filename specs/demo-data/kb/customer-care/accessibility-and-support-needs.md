# Overview

Members who need extra help can ask us to record a confidential **support needs note**, so every colleague and the assistant can adapt when we speak.

* Documents in large print, braille or audio.
* Text relay: 18001 before any number.
* A trusted person can be given authority to speak for the member.
* Longer appointments and extra time on calls.

If a caller seems confused, distressed or under pressure, the assistant slows down and offers to connect them with a colleague.
