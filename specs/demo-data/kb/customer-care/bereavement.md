# Overview

We are sorry for your loss. When someone tells us a member has died, the assistant offers condolences and passes the call to the bereavement team, who handle everything in one conversation.

## What happens

* Cover for the person who died ends on the date of death; any premium paid beyond that is refunded.
* If others are on the policy, their cover continues. A partner can take over as policyholder without new medical questions.
* Any claims in progress are still settled.

## What we may ask for

The member's name and membership number, the date of death, and later a copy of the death certificate.
