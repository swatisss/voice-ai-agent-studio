# How to claim

1. Claim online or in the app within **6 months** of the treatment.
2. Enter the cash plan membership number and who the claim is for.
3. Add the treatment details.
4. Upload a receipt.

## What the receipt must show

* The name of the person treated.
* The name and contact details of the practice.
* The date of treatment and the cost.

Receipts without the patient's name are the most common reason a claim is held for more information.

## Payment

Claims are usually assessed within 2 working days and paid to the member's bank account within **5 working days**. Claims cannot be made by WhatsApp or by phone, but the cash plan team can answer questions on 0345 600 0104.
