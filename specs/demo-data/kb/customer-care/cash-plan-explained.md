# Overview

A cash plan pays money back for everyday healthcare costs, up to a yearly limit for each benefit. **Pre-existing conditions are covered.** Limits reset at the start of each benefit year.

## Levels and yearly limits

| Benefit | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dental (check-ups and treatment) | £200 | £300 | £400 |
| Optical (glasses, lenses, eye tests) | £60 | £120 | £200 |
| Therapies (physio, osteopathy, chiropractic) | £100 | £200 | £300 |
| Specialist consultations and diagnostics | £150 | £250 | £350 |
| Hospital nights (£20 a night, up to 20 nights) | £400 | £600 | £800 |

Monthly prices start from about £6 for Level 1. Partners and children up to 18 can be added.

## Paid in full up to the limit

Claims are refunded at 100% of the cost, up to the remaining limit for that benefit.
