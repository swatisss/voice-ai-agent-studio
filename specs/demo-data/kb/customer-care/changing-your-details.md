# Overview

Verified members can change their **email address, mobile number and postal address** with the assistant. New details are read back before they are saved, and a confirmation is sent.

## Bank details

Bank details are changed only through a secure link (see Premiums and Direct Debit).

## Changes that need a colleague

* Adding a partner or another adult, or removing someone from the policy.
* A change of name.
* Changing products, excess, outpatient limit or hospital list mid-term (usually done at renewal).
* Moving abroad.

## Who can make changes

The policyholder can change anything on the policy. Adult members on a family policy can update their own contact details.
