# Overview

When you receive care, your provider sends a claim to Evergreen Health. You can ask about any claim by its claim number, which appears on your Explanation of Benefits (EOB).

## Processing

The claim was received and is being reviewed. Most claims are decided within 30 days of receipt.

## Paid

The plan paid its share. Your EOB shows what the plan paid and what you owe the provider. Wait for the provider's bill before paying.

## Pending information

The plan needs more information, usually from your provider (for example an authorization number or medical records). The claim continues once the information arrives; your provider is notified directly.

## Denied

The plan did not pay the claim. The EOB lists the reason. You have the right to appeal within 180 days of the denial notice. Appeals are handled by an appeals specialist.

## Explanation of Benefits

An EOB is not a bill. It explains how a claim was processed. EOBs are available in the member portal and are mailed if you chose paper statements.
