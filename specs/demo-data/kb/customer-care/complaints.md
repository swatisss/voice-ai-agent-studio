# Overview

Complaints are taken seriously and help us improve. The assistant does not resolve complaints itself: it listens, summarises the complaint and hands over to the customer relations team with the details, so the member does not have to repeat themselves.

## Timescales

* Acknowledgment within **5 working days**.
* Many complaints are resolved within 3 working days with a summary resolution letter.
* A final response within **8 weeks**.

## Financial Ombudsman Service

If the member is unhappy with the final response, or has not had one after 8 weeks, they can refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge.
