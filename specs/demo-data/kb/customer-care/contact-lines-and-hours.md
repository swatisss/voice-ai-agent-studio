# Overview

One number now reaches every team: the virtual assistant asks what you need and connects you. The direct lines below still work.

| Team | Number | Open |
|---|---|---|
| Member services (existing customers) | 0345 600 0101 | Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 8am to 4pm, bank holidays 8am to 1pm |
| Health insurance quotes (new customers) | 0800 600 0102 | Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 9am to 1pm |
| Claims and pre-authorisation | 0345 600 0103 | Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm, Saturday 8am to 4pm |
| Cash plan | 0345 600 0104 | Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, Saturday 8am to 1pm |
| 24-hour nurse line | 0345 600 0105 | 24 hours a day, 7 days a week |

Renewal queries: Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm and Saturday 9am to 12.30pm.

## Calling from abroad

Add +44 and drop the first 0 of the number.

## Text relay

Customers with hearing or speech difficulties can use Relay UK: dial 18001 followed by the number.

## Online

The Northgate app and online account let members view pre-authorisations, request treatment, book Digital GP appointments and download documents. Phone calls may be recorded or monitored.
