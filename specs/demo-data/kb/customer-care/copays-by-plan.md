# Overview

A copay is a flat amount you pay for certain visits. Copays apply even before you meet your deductible.

## Evergreen Silver PPO

Primary care $30 · Specialist $35 · Urgent care $60 · Emergency room $250 · Telehealth $0.

## Evergreen Gold HMO

Primary care $20 · Specialist $40 (referral from your primary care doctor required) · Urgent care $50 · Emergency room $200 · Telehealth $0.

## Evergreen Bronze EPO

Primary care $45 · Specialist $75 · Urgent care $90 · Emergency room $400 · Telehealth $15.

## Emergency room copays

The emergency room copay is waived if you are admitted to the hospital from the emergency room.
