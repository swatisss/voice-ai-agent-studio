# Overview

Benefits and annual limits depend on the plan. Limits are per person per plan year.

## Benefits

* **Inpatient care** — covered with no annual limit; pre-authorization required.
* **Outpatient care** — Silver $5,000 (10% co-pay), Gold $8,000 (no co-pay), Bronze $2,000 (20% co-pay).
* **Maternity** — Silver $10,000, Gold $15,000, Bronze $5,000; 12-month waiting period; pre-authorization required.
* **Dental** — Silver $800 (20% co-pay), Gold $1,500 (10% co-pay); not covered on Bronze.
* **Optical** — Silver $300, Gold $500; not covered on Bronze.
* **Mental health** — Silver $4,000, Gold $6,000, Bronze $2,000.
* **Physiotherapy** — Silver $1,200, Gold $2,000, Bronze $600; a referral is needed.

## Checking what is left

A verified member can ask how much of a limit is used and what remains. Amounts update when claims are processed.

## Emergencies

Emergency care is covered from the first day and is never subject to waiting periods.
