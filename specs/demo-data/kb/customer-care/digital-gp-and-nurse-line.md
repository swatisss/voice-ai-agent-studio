# Digital GP

Health insurance members and everyone on their policy can book **video or phone appointments** with a GP or an advanced nurse practitioner at no extra cost.

* Available **6am to 10pm on weekdays** and **8am to 10pm at weekends and bank holidays**.
* Book through the app, online, or with the assistant.
* A text with the video link arrives 15 minutes before; for phone appointments the clinician calls the member's mobile.
* The GP can issue private prescriptions and an **open referral** to a specialist, which the claims team uses for pre-authorisation.

Digital GP is not included with a cash plan on its own.

# 24-hour nurse line

Members and their immediate family can call a nurse any time of day or night: 0345 600 0105.

# Emergencies

For chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke, severe bleeding or a serious injury, call **999** or go to A&E. Private health insurance does not cover emergency care in A&E.
