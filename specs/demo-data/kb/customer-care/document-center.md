# Overview

Policyholders can request their policy documents from the document center. A verified caller can have them sent during the call.

## Documents

* **Policy schedule** — the summary of your cover, limits and premium.
* **Policy wording** — the full terms and conditions.
* **Membership card** — your health insurance card (health policies).
* **Certificate of insurance** — proof of cover (motor policies).
* **Premium invoice** — invoice and payment history.
* **Annual premium statement** — total premiums paid in the year (health policies).
* **Green Card** — international motor insurance certificate (motor policies); see the Green Card article.

## Delivery options

* **Email** — to the address on file, ready within 15 minutes.
* **Download link** — instant, from the member portal, available for 7 days.
* **Post** — to the address on file in 5 to 7 business days. Not available for the Green Card.

## Security

Documents are only sent to the contact details on file and only after identity verification. They cannot be sent to a new address by phone.
