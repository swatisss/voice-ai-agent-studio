# Overview

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| Membership certificate | Who is covered, the options chosen and any personal exclusions |
| Policy guide | The full terms and conditions |
| Digital membership card | Membership number for hospitals and specialists; also in the app |
| Premium statement | Premiums paid in the year |
| Table of benefits (cash plan) | Cash plan limits for each benefit |

## Delivery

* **Email**: within 15 minutes.
* **Text message**: a secure link within 5 minutes.
* **Post**: 5 to 7 working days.

The assistant never reads out the email address or address on file.
