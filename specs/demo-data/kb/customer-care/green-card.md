# Overview

The **Green Card** is the international motor insurance certificate. It proves your motor policy is valid when you drive in other countries.

## Who can get one

Holders of an **active** Evergreen Motor Comprehensive policy. Health policies do not have a Green Card.

## Covered countries

Austria, Belgium, Denmark, France, Germany, Greece, Ireland, Italy, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland and Turkey.

## Validity

A Green Card is valid for the travel dates you give, up to a maximum of **90 days**. The travel start date can be today or up to 60 days ahead.

## How to get it

Request it during the call: tell us the countries and the travel dates. It is issued immediately and sent by **email** or as a **download link**. It cannot be posted.

## Good to know

* Carry a printed or digital copy while driving abroad.
* For countries not on the list, or trips longer than 90 days, a specialist must assess the request.
* If the policy is not active, a Green Card cannot be issued until payment is up to date.
