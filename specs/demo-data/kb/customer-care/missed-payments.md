# Overview

If a monthly payment is missed, **cover for the member and everyone on the policy is suspended** until it is paid. Claims and pre-authorisations cannot be approved while cover is suspended.

## Getting cover back

Pay the missed amount within **30 days** of the failed collection and cover continues with no gap:

* **Retry the Direct Debit**: the assistant can arrange a new collection at least 3 working days ahead.
* **Pay by card**: the assistant sends a secure payment link by text or email. Card details are never taken on the call.

## After 30 days

If the missed amount is not paid within 30 days, the policy is cancelled from the date of the missed payment. Rejoining means new underwriting.

## Why payments fail

Usually insufficient funds, a cancelled Direct Debit instruction or a changed bank account. If the bank account changed, send a secure link to update it before retrying.
