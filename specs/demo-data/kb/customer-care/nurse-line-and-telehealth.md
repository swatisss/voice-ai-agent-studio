# Overview

Evergreen Health members can talk to a registered nurse any time, and can see a doctor by video for many common conditions.

## 24/7 nurse line

Call 1-800-555-0142, any time, day or night, for health questions and advice on where to get care. There is no cost.

## Telehealth visits

Video visits are available for common conditions like colds, rashes and minor infections, and for many behavioral health visits. Telehealth costs $0 on Silver PPO and Gold HMO plans and $15 on the Bronze EPO plan.

## Emergencies

For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For a mental health crisis, call or text 988.
