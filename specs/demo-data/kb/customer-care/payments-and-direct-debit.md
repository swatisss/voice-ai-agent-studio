# Overview

Premiums are paid **monthly by Direct Debit**. Annual payment by Direct Debit is also available and works out the same price.

## Collection day

The Direct Debit is collected on the same day each month, from the 1st to the 28th. Members can change the collection day by phone; the change applies from the next month. Allow at least 7 days before the next payment for any change.

## Changing bank details

For security, bank details are **never taken over the phone**. The assistant sends a secure link by text or email that is valid for 24 hours; the member enters the new account details there.

## Price changes

The premium can change only at renewal. Adding a person or an add-on mid-term changes it from the next collection.

## Premium statements

A yearly premium statement is available for tax or employer purposes and can be sent by email, text link or post.

## Direct Debit Guarantee

Payments are protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee: if an error is made, the member is entitled to a full and immediate refund from their bank.
