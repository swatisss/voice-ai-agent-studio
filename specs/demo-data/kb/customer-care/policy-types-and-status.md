# Overview

Evergreen Health offers **health** policies (Silver PPO, Gold HMO, Bronze EPO) and **motor** policies (Motor Comprehensive). A member can hold both.

## Policy statuses

* **Active** — cover is in force and premiums are up to date.
* **Pending start** — the policy has been bought but its start date has not arrived yet.
* **Active, payment overdue** — a payment was missed; cover continues during the grace period.
* **Lapsed** — the grace period ended without payment; cover has stopped. A lapsed policy can be reinstated by a specialist.
* **Cancelled** or **expired** — the policy ended and is no longer in force.

## Grace period

If a monthly payment is missed, cover continues for **14 days**. Paying within the grace period keeps the policy active with no gap.

## Renewals

Policies renew on the end date of the cover period. A renewal notice with the new premium is sent about 45 days before. Policies renew automatically unless the policyholder declines or changes them.
