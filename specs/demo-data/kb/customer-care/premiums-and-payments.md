# Overview

The annual premium can be paid **monthly** or **annually**. Paying annually earns a **5% discount** compared with paying monthly.

## Payment methods

Card, direct debit or bank transfer. The method on file is used for monthly payments and renewals.

## Payment dates

Monthly payments are taken on the same day each month. The next payment date is shown in the member portal and can be read out by the assistant for a verified member.

## Late payments

A missed payment starts a **14-day grace period**. Reminders are sent by email and text. If payment is not received before the grace period ends, the policy lapses.

## Receipts and invoices

Premium invoices and receipts are available from the document center and the member portal.

## Changing the payment method or date

A specialist can change the payment method or date. The assistant cannot take card details by phone.
