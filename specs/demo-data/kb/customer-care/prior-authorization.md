# Overview

Prior authorization (PA) means Evergreen Health must approve certain services before you receive them for them to be covered.

## Services that usually need prior authorization

Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET scans), planned inpatient surgeries, durable medical equipment such as CPAP machines, home health care, and some specialty drugs.

## Who requests it

Your provider submits the request with your medical information. You do not need to submit anything yourself.

## Decision times

Standard requests are decided within 7 to 14 days. Urgent requests are decided within 72 hours. Your provider is notified of the decision, and you receive a letter.

## Checking status

A specialist can look up the status of an authorization by its number, which starts with "PA-".
