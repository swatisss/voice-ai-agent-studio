# Renewal

Policies renew automatically each year. At least **28 days** before renewal, the member receives the renewal price and any changes. Prices change mainly because of age, rising medical costs and the claims made in the year.

Options can be changed at renewal: a higher excess, a lower outpatient limit or a different hospital list can reduce the price.

# Cooling-off

New members have **21 days** from the start date (or from receiving the documents, if later) to cancel with a **full refund**, as long as no claim has been made.

# Cancelling

Members can cancel at any time by speaking to a colleague; the assistant hands over with a summary. After cooling-off, cover ends at the end of the month already paid for, and there is no refund for months already covered.
