# Overview

A waiting period is the time after the policy starts before a benefit can be claimed.

## Waiting periods

* Inpatient, outpatient, mental health, physiotherapy: none.
* Optical: 30 days.
* Dental: 90 days on Silver, 60 days on Gold.
* Maternity: 365 days.

Emergency care has no waiting period.

## Pre-existing conditions

Conditions that existed before the policy started are covered after **12 months of continuous cover**.

## Not covered

Cosmetic procedures, experimental or unproven treatments, and treatment outside the policy's network except in an emergency.

## Pre-authorization

Inpatient and maternity care need pre-authorization. The provider requests it; members do not need to submit anything.
