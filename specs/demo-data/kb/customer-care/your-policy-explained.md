# Overview

Every member's **membership certificate** lists who is covered, the options chosen and any personal exclusions. The **policy guide** has the full terms.

## Products

* **Northgate Complete** covers diagnosis and treatment: specialist consultations, tests and scans, surgery and hospital stays, cancer care, mental health and therapies.
* **Northgate Essentials** covers treatment once you already have a diagnosis (from the NHS or a self-pay consultation). Outpatient consultations, tests and scans for diagnosis are not included.

## Excess

The excess is the amount you pay towards claims: £0, £100, £250 or £500. It applies **once per person per policy year** and resets at renewal. The hospital or specialist usually asks you to pay it directly.

## Outpatient limit (Complete only)

Outpatient care (consultations, tests, scans and therapies where you do not stay in hospital) is covered up to the yearly limit you chose: unlimited, £1,000, £750 or £500. Treatment in hospital is not counted against it.

## Hospital lists

* **Essential**: about 200 partner hospitals outside central London.
* **Extended**: about 300 hospitals across the UK, outside central London.
* **London Premium**: every recognised hospital, including central London.

Treatment must be at a hospital on your list.

## Underwriting

How your medical history is treated (moratorium, full medical underwriting or switched terms) is shown on the membership certificate. See the claims team for whether a specific condition is covered.

## Add-ons

Dental and optical, Therapies Plus, and worldwide emergency travel cover can be added at renewal or when you join.
