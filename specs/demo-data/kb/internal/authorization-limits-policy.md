# Overview

Approval limits per claim, in USD:

| Role | Inpatient | Outpatient | Dental |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claims handler | 5,000 | 2,000 | 1,000 |
| Senior handler | 25,000 | 10,000 | 5,000 |
| Team leader | 100,000 | 50,000 | 20,000 |
| Claims manager | 250,000 | 100,000 | 50,000 |

## Rules

* Amounts above the role limit go to the next role.
* Above the claims manager limit, the medical director's co-signature is required.
* Never split a claim to stay under a limit.
* Exceptions need the claims manager's written approval.
