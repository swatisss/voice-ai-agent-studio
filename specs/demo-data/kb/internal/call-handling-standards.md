# Overview

* Greet with your name and the company name.
* Verify identity before account detail.
* Keep hold times under **2 minutes**; offer a callback if longer.
* Use warm transfers and summarize the case to the next colleague.
* Document the call within **5 minutes** of finishing.
* Use empathy statements when the customer is upset.
