# Overview

## Steps

1. **Register** the claim and acknowledge it within **2 business days**.
2. **Check eligibility**: policy active on the date of service, premium up to date, benefit and waiting period.
3. **Check pre-authorization** where required (inpatient, maternity, advanced imaging).
4. **Check the provider**: in-network or an emergency exception.
5. **Apply the benefit**: annual limit, deductible, co-pay.
6. **Decide** within **30 days** of receipt (urgent requests within 72 hours) and notify the member and provider.
7. **Document** the decision reason in the claim notes.

## Pending information

Request missing information from the provider first; record the request date. The 30-day clock pauses until it arrives.

## Denials

Always state the specific reason and the appeal right (180 days).
