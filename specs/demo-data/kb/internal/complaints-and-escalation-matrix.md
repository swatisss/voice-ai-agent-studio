# Overview

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction.

## Service levels

* Log within **1 business day**.
* Acknowledge within **2 business days**.
* Resolve within **15 business days**; otherwise escalate to the Customer Resolution Team (ext. 4120).

## Escalation matrix

* Suspected fraud → Special Investigations Unit, ext. 4410.
* Legal threats or solicitor involvement → Legal & Compliance, ext. 4600.
* Regulator mentioned → Legal & Compliance, ext. 4600, same day.
* Data breach or privacy concern → Privacy Office, ext. 4700.
* Clinical questions → Clinical Governance, ext. 4850.

## Vulnerable customers

Prioritize customers who are ill, bereaved or in financial difficulty and note their needs on the account.
