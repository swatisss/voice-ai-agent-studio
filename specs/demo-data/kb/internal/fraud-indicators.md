# Overview

## Common indicators

* Duplicate invoices or the same service billed twice.
* Claims shortly after the policy start date.
* Inconsistent documents, altered dates or amounts.
* A provider billing pattern far above peers.
* Pressure to pay quickly.

## What to do

* Do **not** accuse or alert the customer or provider.
* Record the facts in the claim notes.
* Refer to the Special Investigations Unit, ext. 4410.
