# Overview

An appeal asks Evergreen Health to reconsider a denied claim or authorization. A grievance is a complaint about service or quality of care.

## Deadlines

* You can file an appeal within 180 days of the denial notice.
* Standard appeals are decided within 30 days.
* Expedited appeals, for urgent care where waiting could seriously harm your health, are decided within 72 hours.

## Who files

Appeals and grievances are filed with an appeals specialist, who reviews the claim, explains what documents help, and submits the appeal. The virtual assistant connects callers to a specialist for this.

## Helpful documents

The denial notice or EOB, a letter from your doctor explaining why the care was needed, and any related medical records or bills.

## External review

If an appeal is denied, you may request an independent external review. The specialist explains this option in the appeal decision letter.
