# Overview

Your deductible is the amount you pay for covered care each plan year before your plan starts sharing costs. The plan year runs January 1 to December 31.

## What counts toward the deductible

Most covered services that are not billed as a flat copay count toward your deductible, such as hospital stays, surgery, imaging (MRI, CT) and lab work. Preventive care is covered at no cost and does not require meeting your deductible.

## Deductibles by plan

* Evergreen Silver PPO: $1,500 per person.
* Evergreen Gold HMO: $500 per person.
* Evergreen Bronze EPO: $4,000 per person.

## After you meet your deductible

You pay coinsurance — a share of the cost — and the plan pays the rest, until you reach your out-of-pocket maximum.

## Out-of-pocket maximum

The out-of-pocket maximum is the most you pay in a plan year for covered in-network care. Deductible, copays and coinsurance all count toward it. After you reach it, the plan pays 100% of covered in-network care for the rest of the plan year.

* Silver PPO: $6,000 · Gold HMO: $4,000 · Bronze EPO: $8,500.

## Checking your balance

A verified member can ask how much of their deductible and out-of-pocket maximum they have met. The amounts update when claims are processed, so recent visits may not appear yet.
