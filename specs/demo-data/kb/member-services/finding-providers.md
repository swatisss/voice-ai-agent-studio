# Overview

In-network providers have agreed rates with Evergreen Health, so you pay less. The assistant can search for in-network providers by specialty and zip code.

## Plan types

* PPO: you can see in-network or out-of-network providers; out-of-network care costs more.
* HMO: use in-network providers and get a referral from your primary care doctor before seeing a specialist.
* EPO: use in-network providers; no referrals needed for specialists.

## Before your visit

Confirm the provider is accepting new patients and still in network when you book. Bring your ID card or show the digital card.

## Out-of-network care

Except in emergencies, care from out-of-network providers may not be covered on HMO and EPO plans, and costs more on PPO plans.
