# Overview

Your member ID card shows your member ID, plan, and copays. Providers and pharmacies use it to bill Evergreen Health.

## Digital ID card

A digital ID card is always available in the Evergreen Health app and the member portal, right away, and works the same as the physical card.

## Replacement card

A verified member can request a replacement physical card by phone. Replacement cards arrive by mail in 7 to 10 business days at the address on file. There is no charge.

## New cards

New cards are mailed automatically when you change plans or when a new plan year starts with changed copays.
