# Overview

Register at the member portal or in the Evergreen Health app with your **member ID** and **date of birth**.

## Steps

1. Enter the member ID and date of birth.
2. We send a 6-digit verification code to the email on file; enter it.
3. Choose a password.

## What you can do

View policies and claims, download documents and your membership card, update contact details, and message member services.

## Trouble registering

If the code does not arrive, check the spam folder or ask for a new code. A specialist can help if the email on file is wrong.
