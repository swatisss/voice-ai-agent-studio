# Overview

## Waiting periods

Waiting periods start on your policy start date: optical 30 days, dental 60 or 90 days depending on the plan, maternity 12 months. Emergency care is covered from day one.

## Primary provider

Choose a primary care provider from the directory. On the Gold HMO plan a referral from your primary provider is needed to see a specialist.

## Pre-authorization

Inpatient and maternity care need pre-authorization, which your provider requests.

## Preventive check-up

An annual preventive check-up is covered at no cost and does not use your deductible.
