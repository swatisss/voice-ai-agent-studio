# Overview

Show your membership card at appointments and pharmacies. It lists your member ID, plan and key co-pays.

## Digital card

The digital card is in the app straight away and can be added to your phone wallet.

## Replacement

Request a replacement from the document center: email in 15 minutes, download instantly, or post in 5 to 7 business days.
