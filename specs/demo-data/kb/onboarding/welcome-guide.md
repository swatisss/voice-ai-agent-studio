# Overview

Welcome to Evergreen Health. In your first weeks you will receive a welcome pack by email, your membership card and an invitation to set up your online account.

## Getting help

* Member services: call any time during business hours.
* 24/7 nurse line: **1-800-555-0142**, free for members.
* Telehealth video visits for common conditions.

## What to do first

Confirm your contact details, set up your online account, download your membership card, choose your primary provider and read about waiting periods.
