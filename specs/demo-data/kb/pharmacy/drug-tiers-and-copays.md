# Overview

The formulary is the list of covered drugs. Each drug is placed in a tier that sets your copay.

## Tiers (30-day supply at an in-network pharmacy)

* Tier 1 — preferred generics: $5.
* Tier 2 — preferred brands: $40.
* Tier 3 — non-preferred brands: $75.
* Specialty tier — complex or high-cost drugs: $150.

## Mail order

A 90-day supply through Evergreen Mail Pharmacy costs two times the 30-day copay.
