# Overview

Some drugs need approval before they are covered, or are limited to certain quantities.

## Prior authorization for drugs

Your prescriber submits the request. Decisions are made within 72 hours, or 24 hours for urgent requests.

## Quantity limits

Some drugs are covered only up to a set amount per fill, for safety or cost reasons.

## Specialty pharmacy

Specialty-tier drugs are filled through Evergreen Specialty Pharmacy, which ships to your home and offers pharmacist support.
