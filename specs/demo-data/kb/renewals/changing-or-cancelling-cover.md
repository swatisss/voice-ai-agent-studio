# Overview

## At renewal

Cover level, payment frequency and voluntary excess can be changed.

## Mid-term changes

Mid-term changes (adding a person, changing address or vehicle) are handled by a specialist.

## Cancelling

* Within the **14-day cooling-off** period after renewal: full refund if no claim was made.
* Later: pro-rata refund of unused cover, less any claims paid.
* Cancellation requests are handled by a specialist; the assistant records a callback and does not cancel policies.
