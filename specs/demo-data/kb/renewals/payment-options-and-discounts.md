# Overview

## Options at renewal

* **Monthly payments** — spread across the year, no interest.
* **Annual payment** — one payment, **5% discount**.
* **Motor voluntary excess** — a higher excess lowers the premium (for example $500).

## How the payment is taken

The saved payment method is used on the dates in the renewal schedule. Changes to the payment method are made by a specialist, not by phone card entry with the assistant.

## Accepting a renewal

Accepting records the decision; no payment is taken during the call.
