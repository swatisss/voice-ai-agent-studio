# Overview

Premiums are reviewed every year.

## Health policies

* **Medical cost inflation** — the cost of care rises each year (currently about 3.2%).
* **Age band** — premiums step up when a member moves into an older age band.
* Making a claim does **not** raise a health premium by itself.

## Motor policies

* **Repair cost inflation** — parts and labor costs.
* **Claims history** — a claim in the last 12 months can increase the premium.

## Ways to reduce the premium

* Pay annually: **5% discount**.
* Motor: raise the voluntary excess (for example to $500).
* Review the cover level at renewal.
