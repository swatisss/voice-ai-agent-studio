# Overview

About **45 days** before the end of the cover period, Evergreen Health sends a renewal notice with the new premium and any changes.

## Automatic renewal

Policies renew automatically on the renewal date. The saved payment method is used unless the policyholder declines or changes the policy first.

## Renewal documents

The renewal schedule and updated documents are emailed about 30 days before the renewal date.

## Changes at renewal

Cover level, payment frequency and (for motor) the voluntary excess can be changed at renewal without penalty.

## Cooling-off

A renewed policy can be cancelled within **14 days** for a full refund if no claim has been made.
