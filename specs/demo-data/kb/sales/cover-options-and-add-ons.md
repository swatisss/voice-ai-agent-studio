# Excess

£0, £100, £250 or £500, paid once per person per policy year. A higher excess lowers the price: roughly 6% less at £100, 13% less at £250 and 22% less at £500 compared with no excess.

# Outpatient limit (Complete)

Unlimited, £1,000, £750 or £500 a year for consultations, tests, scans and therapies outside hospital. A £1,000 limit is about 12% cheaper than unlimited.

# Hospital list

* **Essential**: about 200 partner hospitals outside central London (about 15% cheaper).
* **Extended**: about 300 hospitals across the UK, outside central London.
* **London Premium**: every recognised hospital including central London (about 20% more).

# Add-ons (per person per month)

* **Dental and optical**: £11.50. One check-up a year plus a £300 dental and £150 optical allowance.
* **Therapies Plus**: £6. Unlimited physiotherapy, osteopathy and chiropractic sessions when clinically needed.
* **Travel cover**: £4.50. Worldwide emergency medical cover for trips up to 30 days.
