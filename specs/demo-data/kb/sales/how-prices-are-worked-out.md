# What affects the price

* **Age** of each person (children under 18 have a lower child rate).
* **Where you live**: prices are higher in London and the South East, where treatment costs more.
* **Smoking**: anyone who has smoked or used nicotine in the last 12 months pays about 10% more.
* **Cover options**: product, excess, outpatient limit, hospital list and add-ons.
* **Underwriting**: full medical underwriting is about 5% cheaper than moratorium because exclusions are known up front.

# Discounts

* Two people on one policy: 5% off. Three or more: 10% off.

# The quote

Prices are monthly by Direct Debit. A quote is valid for **14 days**. Prices are confirmed after the underwriting questions. Prices rise at renewal mainly with age and medical inflation.
