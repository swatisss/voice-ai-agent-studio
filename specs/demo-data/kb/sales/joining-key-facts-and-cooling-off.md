# Before you buy

The assistant reads the **key facts**: the product and options chosen, the excess, any exclusions, the monthly price, and the 21-day cooling-off period. Then it reads the **declaration**: the information you gave is true and complete to the best of your knowledge, and anything you did not tell us may mean a claim is not paid. You must say yes to both.

This is a **non-advised** sale: we give you facts so you can choose, not a personal recommendation.

# After you buy

* You get a **policy number** and a **membership number** straight away.
* Your welcome pack (membership certificate, policy guide and key facts) is sent by email or text, as you choose.
* A **secure link** to set up the Direct Debit is sent by email or text. Bank details are never taken on the phone. Set it up within 7 days.
* The first payment is taken about 10 days after cover starts.

# Cooling-off

You can cancel within **21 days** for a full refund, as long as you have not made a claim.
