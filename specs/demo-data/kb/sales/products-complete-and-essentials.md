# Northgate Complete

Covers the journey from diagnosis to treatment: specialist consultations, tests and scans, surgery and hospital stays, cancer care (including advanced drugs not routinely funded), mental health support, therapies such as physiotherapy, and Digital GP. The outpatient limit can be unlimited, £1,000, £750 or £500.

# Northgate Essentials

A lower-cost option that covers treatment **once you have a diagnosis** (from the NHS or a self-pay consultation): surgery, hospital stays, cancer treatment and Digital GP. Outpatient consultations, tests and scans for diagnosis are not included.

# Never covered

Emergency care in A&E, normal pregnancy and childbirth, cosmetic treatment, long-term (chronic) conditions once they are stable, and conditions you had before joining unless your underwriting allows it.

# Included with both

A 24-hour nurse line, Digital GP appointments, Direct Access to specialists for muscle, bone and joint problems, mental health and cancer symptoms (Complete) and a mental health support line.
