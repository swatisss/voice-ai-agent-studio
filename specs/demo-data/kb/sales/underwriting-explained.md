# Moratorium

No medical questions when you join. Any condition you had **symptoms, treatment, medication or advice for in the 5 years before** cover starts is excluded, until you have had **2 clear years** on cover with no symptoms, treatment or advice for it. Then it can be covered. Before a claim, you fill in a short pre-treatment form.

# Full medical underwriting

You answer questions about your medical history for everyone on the policy. We tell you up front which conditions are excluded, and they are listed on the membership certificate. Claims are usually quicker because there are no surprises. It is about 5% cheaper than moratorium. Serious conditions may need an underwriter to review the application before cover can start.

# Switching from another insurer

If you have had at least **12 months of continuous cover** with another UK insurer and switch with no break, you can keep your existing underwriting terms or moratorium start date. You need to live in the UK at least 6 months a year, be registered with a UK GP and not play semi-professional or professional sport.

# Choosing

The assistant explains the options but does not recommend one: this is a non-advised service.
