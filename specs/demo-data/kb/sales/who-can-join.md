# Ages

The policyholder must be **18 to 79** when cover starts. Partners can be included, and children can stay on a family policy up to age 25.

# How many people

Up to 6 people on one policy.

# Residence

Everyone must live in the UK for at least 6 months a year and be registered with a UK GP.

# Business cover

Businesses with 2 to 249 employees, and corporate schemes of 50 or more, are handled by the business team; the assistant hands over.
