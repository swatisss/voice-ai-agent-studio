## 2026-11-04 (voice playback jitter buffer)
* **Update** to the [voice protocol](api/voice-protocol.md) browser client requirements: when nothing is queued (a reply starts, or delivery fell behind playback) the next audio buffer starts 200 ms after now instead of 20 ms. With only 20 ms in hand, any network or CPU delay was heard as the agent's voice breaking mid-sentence in Talk mode (seen in a dental demo rehearsal on 2026-11-04). Replies start about 0.2 s later; barge-in still discards queued audio at once. `apps/web/lib/voice.ts` (`PREBUFFER_S`).
